The game

Find your way
through the depths.

Realms of Fate adds player classes, mana-powered wands and custom currencies to the installed Delver game.

Choose Mage or Fighter, cycle the spells carried by your wand, and customize definitions through JSON. Skill trees and crafting are future work.

I

Weapons & magic

Choose a class and use Bolt or Heal through an item-bound spell list, with icons and a blue mana meter.

II

Build your own content

Edit spell JSON, draw original PNG icons, and configure currency drops and shops.

III

Explore the dungeon

Discover secrets and dungeon areas, with ambient effects that give each space its own mood.
